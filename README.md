# Machine Economy Lab — Berlin

## About

The Machine Economy Lab is a student-led research group at HTW Berlin,
supervised by Prof. Dr. Alexandra Mikityuk. The group investigates how
AI agents, blockchain technology, and secure M2M connectivity can enable
autonomous machines to participate in economic transactions — without
human intervention.

The foundation of this research group is **RoboPay** — an idea conceived
and developed by Kevin Ehrentraut starting in 2025, beginning with two
whitepapers and evolving into a working hardware PoC with on-chain proof
on Solana Devnet.

## Research Question

How can AI agents and blockchain-based payment protocols enable trustless,
autonomous economic transactions between machines and humans — and what
are the technical, ethical, and societal implications?

## Scope

- Comparison of GPS-triggered payments vs. AI Agent x402 payments for autonomous delivery
- Design and implementation of a physical hardware prototype (e.g. drone + autonomous delivery vehicle)
- SWOT analysis of Machine Economy adoption
- Risk assessment: machine autonomy, AI hallucinations, human control boundaries
- Societal impact: how will Machine Economy change human payment behaviour?

## Methods

Case Study · Experiment · Questionnaire · Expert Interviews · Systematic Literature Review

## Goal

A working hardware prototype of two communicating autonomous machines —
e.g. a drone triggers the opening of a letterbox via Staex MCC tunnel
upon arrival, while the payment settlement happens autonomously on-chain
via Solana. All members contribute directly to the design, development
and testing of this prototype — presented at a blockchain/AI/robotics
conference and submitted to:

- Colosseum Solana Hackathon (Oct/Nov 2026)
- Superteam Germany Ideathon (parallel to Colosseum)
- A relevant conference in AI, Blockchain, or Robotics
